Accounting group vs Payment Method report

How to Use This Report

  1. Upload Files: Select your Transactions (CSV 1) and Payments (CSV 2).
  2. Apply Filters: Use the dropdowns to filter by Group, Tax Name, or Payment Method.
  3. View Results:
    • Table 1 (Sales): Tax summary (TaxName, PreTax, Tax).
    • Table 2 (Payments): Total payment per Account.
    • Table 3 (Matched Accounts): Tax summary for Accounts present in both reports.
    • Table 4 (Items): Item totals for those matched accounts.
  4. Data Inclusion: All rows matching your filters are included.
